**INTRODUCTION TO NATOMA** 

* Natoma was set up by an ex-Okta founding team originally as an identity solution for non-human identities \- agents/bots etc  
* Feedback from SNP Global that while NHI was a problem, the rollout of AI agents with little visibility around which agent was talking to who became a bigger problem \- pointed Natoma to start thinking about visibility and control  
* The introduction of MCP as a protocol by Anthropic came with their elegant architecture \- also allowing Natoma to help AI systems connect to their internal data in a safe, secure way, especially given their Okta background  
* Natoma moved from pureplay NHI to pitch 3 buckets of solutions to customers \- NHI discovery, NHI lifecycle management and MCP gateway  
* The real turning point came when every single customer expressed immediate interest in the 3rd bucket \- MCP gateway at the expense of the first two. In 2 months Natoma had more design partners for MCP gateway than for the other two solutions  
* Natoma completely pivoted from NHI to MCP gateways  
* Initial FOMO that they may be late to the party was assuaged when they saw most enterprises were still locking down on AI enablement  
* The product build was philosophically Enterprise focussed \- build coverage first, build all the features/bells and whistles that enterprises would want to see and then improve
